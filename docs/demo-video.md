## Rules for the cut
- Under 3 minutes, sound on, no music with a license that cannot be shown.
- The scenario in the README ("picture a student who...") is labelled as a scenario everywhere it appears. No invented testimonials and no invented numbers.
- Both citations are shown on screen in short form the first time they are spoken.
- If a ghost window or an animated favicon that follows the person into other tabs is built before recording, it joins the 0:24 beat; until then that beat is the tab title, the rattle and the sound.
