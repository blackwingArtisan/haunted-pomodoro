# Demo video plan (under 3 minutes)

Recorded from `/?demo=1` at 1440 x 900 with sound on. Every number on screen is a real count taken at recording time; nothing is mocked. The first line is the one meant to be retold.

| Time | Beat | On screen | Voice-over and sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:10 | Cold open: the stance | The fully haunted parlor: cold blue light, cobwebs, the portrait's eyes following the cursor. | "Forest kills your tree when you look away. This ghost just keeps your candle lit." Then one sourced number: the average stretch on one screen is about 47 seconds (Gloria Mark). |
| 0:10-0:22 | Costume reveal | The drapes part and **Light a candle** is struck: the flame flares and color starts to seep back into the room. | "It's a real Pomodoro timer, wearing a haunted house." A match strike, then a soft organ note. |
| 0:22-0:45 | The haunting leaves the tab | Switch to another app. The tab title reads "Come back…" and the ghost moans; the ghost's floating window follows once the `ghost-leaves-the-tab` spec lands. | A faint moan and a rattle. |
| 0:45-0:58 | Forgiveness | Come back. "There you are. I kept your candle lit." The wander count ticks up, and nothing is lost. | "It never punishes. It just notices." One line on the finding: students who forgave themselves for procrastinating procrastinated less next time (Wohl, Pychyl and Bennett, 2010). |
| 0:58-1:12 | The money shot | The session ends: a chime, embers, the restoration sweep, the portrait closes its eyes, RESTORED / The Parlor. Hold it for 3 seconds. | Silence, then a warm chord. |

| 1:42-2:30 | Built with Kiro | Screen recording in Kiro, not slides: the "Never punish" steering rule, the never-punish guard firing on a save to `src/core/house.ts` and asking for a test, a Kiro-written spec checking off its tasks, the suite going green. | One line on screen: "N unit + M end-to-end tests, 0 axe violations, built across K Kiro sessions", with the real counts. |
| 2:30-2:50 | Close | Pull back to the restored house exterior, its windows glowing. "Free. Your data stays on your device." | "Haunted Pomodoro. Focus by candlelight." End by 2:55. |

## Rules for the cut
- Under 3 minutes, sound on, no music with a license that cannot be shown.
- The scenario in the README ("picture a student who...") is labelled as a scenario everywhere it appears. No invented testimonials.
- Both citations are shown on screen in short form the first time they are spoken.
