# Demo video plan (under 3 minutes)

Recorded from `/?demo=1` at 1440 x 900 with sound on. Demo mode runs a 20 second focus and a 10 second break in a parlor that is one candle from restored.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | The haunted parlor, portrait eyes following the cursor | Most of us lose focus to another tab dozens of times a day, and most timers just keep counting. This one lives in a haunted house. |
| 0:15 | Press **Light a candle**; the flame ignites and starts to melt | Every focus session lights a candle. As it burns, the room's color comes back and the cobwebs thin. |
| 0:35 | Switch to another tab; the tab title reads "Come back…" and the ghost moans | Wander off and the ghost wakes. It drifts closer, rattles the frames and calls you back. |
| 0:50 | Return; the ghost speaks: "There you are. I kept your candle lit." | It never scolds. It counts the wandering and keeps your candle lit. |
| 1:05 | Session ends; chime, Restored banner, portrait closes its eyes, embers rise | Four candles restore a room. The portrait finally rests. |
| 1:25 | Break: the house breathes, UI turns ghost green | Breaks let the house breathe. Long breaks come every four sessions. |
| 1:40 | The Hearth panel, then the House map with six rooms | The streak forgives a missed day, and nothing you restore is ever taken away. Six rooms per house. |
| 2:05 | Keyboard shortcuts, settings with reduced motion, offline reload | It is a real timer: shortcuts, custom lengths, synthesized sound, reduced motion, and it works offline with your data in your browser. |
| 2:30 | `.kiro` folder: steering, EARS specs, hooks | Built with Kiro from steering rules like "never punish" and specs with numbered requirements. |
| 2:45 | Back to the restored parlor | Haunted Pomodoro: focus by candlelight. |
