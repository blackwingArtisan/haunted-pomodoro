# What works today

Every feature of Haunted Pomodoro, as built and tested. The [README](../README.md) is the short tour; [DEVELOPING.md](DEVELOPING.md) covers the build, the tests and the architecture.

- **A real Pomodoro timer.** Configurable focus, short and long breaks, long break every N sessions, auto-start options, pause, resume, skip and reset. Time is kept as an absolute end time, so background tabs never drift, and a reload resumes exactly where you were.
- **Candles and rooms.** The drapes part over a brass title card when the house opens. Starting focus strikes a candle with a warm flare; it melts as the session runs while a pool of candlelight spreads over the wall and the rug, the room's color returns and the cobwebs thin. A haunted room keeps dark edges and the odd flash of lightning in the window. Four finished candles restore a room: the portrait stops staring and closes its eyes, the cobwebs fall, the clock starts ticking, and a band of warm light sweeps the wallpaper while the dust bursts off the room. The house map opens on the exterior, one glowing window per restored room. Six rooms per house, each with its own prop (grandfather clock, library, conservatory, piano, rocking horse, telescope) and, after the Parlor, its own set dressing: a reading chair, a glass roof and vines, a chandelier and music stand, a mobile and toy blocks, an observatory dome full of stars.
- **The ghost.** Switch tabs during focus (Page Visibility API) and the ghost wakes after a short grace period, drifts closer (by the third wandering it fills the room and its green glow gathers at the edges), rattles the room and changes the tab title to call you back. Come back and it says something kind, and the timer reads "Came back 1 time". Comebacks and the time away are counted per session and per day.
- **A story to restore.** The ghost is the house's last owner, who never finished her book. Each restored room gives back a page of it in the ghost's journal (in the house map); six rooms finish the book, she rests, and a new house begins. She has fifteen things to say when you come back, chosen by the room and how often you have wandered, and none of them scold.
- **Will-o'-wisps.** Each time you wander off and come back, the ghost leaves a small light near the ceiling: one per return that day, up to eight. They stay through the end of the session and a reload, and they are never taken away.
- **Unmask.** Press U, or the Unmask button above the room: the drapes close, the ghost tugs her sheet, and they open on a plain Pomodoro (instantly under reduced motion). The timer drops its costume too: no glow, no brass, tiles sized to their content. It is the same timer, tasks and history, each number labelled with the part of the house that stands for it. Press it again and the sheet drops back over her: the house returns exactly where it was, with a candle strike.
- **Breaks let the house breathe.** The room slowly swells and the curtains sway; the UI turns ghost-green.
- **The Hearth.** A streak that forgives one missed day between active days and never takes away a restored room. Fourteen-day candle chart, today's minutes, comebacks today, best streak, a diary line in the ghost's voice ("Yesterday you lit three candles and left me twice. I counted the books while I waited.") and a weekly reflection ("This week: 11 candles, 4 h 35 min focused, 6 comebacks; time away fell from 14 min to 6 min."). It counts comebacks, not distractions, and mentions time away only when it went down. **Save this week's postcard** draws a 1200x630 PNG to share: the house from outside with a lamp in every restored window, the week's candles and comebacks, and a line from the ghost.
- **Task ledger.** Add tasks with planned candles, pick the active one, see candles spent, mark done, clear finished.
- **Sound, synthesized live.** Candle strike, bell chime, restoration arpeggio, ghost moan, rattle, break breath and optional ambient wind, all generated with Web Audio. Volume slider and mute on the main screen.
- **Sound check.** `/sound-check.html` renders every sound offline at full volume, draws its waveform, shows its peak, loudness and fade-out, and has a Play button for each. Every sound must peak between -28 and -1 dBFS and fade cleanly; the test suite enforces it. (This check caught a rattle that peaked at only -31 dBFS; it now uses white noise and peaks near -15.)
- **Fits the first view.** On desktop the stage scales with the window height, so the room, the timer and the Light a candle button are all visible at 1280x720 and up, with or without the demo banner.
- **Keyboard first.** Space, R, S, N, M, A, H, U, comma and ?. All controls are native, labelled and focus-visible; changes are announced in a live region.
- **Reduced motion.** Follows the system setting, or choose reduced or full theatre in settings.
- **Works offline.** Installable PWA with a generated service worker; fonts are self-hosted.
- **Private by default.** Everything lives in localStorage. Optional sync to a Cloudflare Worker is written and tested against a stub, and off unless configured.
- **Export and import.** Settings has "Export house", which downloads `haunted-house-YYYY-MM-DD.json`, and "Import house", which merges a saved file into the current house. The house with more candles wins, so an import never removes a candle or a room. Files over 1 MB, damaged files and files from another format version are refused with a message and change nothing. Both are disabled in demo mode.
- **The ghost follows you.** While a focus candle burns, the tab's icon is a candle drawn on a canvas that melts in 24 steps; while the tab is hidden it is the ghost's face (two drawings that alternate once a second, one still drawing under reduced motion), and in a break it is a candle with a ghost-green flame. The tab title shows the time left and, once the ghost wakes, "Come back… mm:ss left". With **Let her follow me** on (Settings, "The ghost", off by default), pressing Light a candle also opens a small always-on-top Document Picture-in-Picture window with the ghost, the time left as a `role="timer"` and one of six kind lines; she is at full strength while you are away and fades to 25% over 600 ms when you return (at once under reduced motion). Closing the window changes nothing, the next window waits for the next candle, and a refused window is said once in the live region and in Settings. Where the browser has no Document Picture-in-Picture (Firefox, Safari), the switch is disabled with a note and the icon and title do the following. Tested in Playwright with a stand-in window; a manual check in a real Chrome window is still to be recorded here.
- **Optional phase-end notification.** Off by default. "Notify me when a phase ends while this tab is hidden" asks for browser permission when you turn it on, turns itself off with a reason if permission is denied, and shows one notification per phase end only while the tab is hidden.
- **Demo mode.** `/?demo=1` runs a 20 second focus and a 10 second break in a demo house whose fully haunted parlor is restored by a single candle, without touching your own data. `/?demo=finale` starts in the house's last dark room: one session finishes the book, and the house map opens on the last page with a new house down the lane. `/?demo=week` opens a house with two made-up weeks of history, so the weekly reflection, the 14-day chart and the postcard have something to show.
