# What app reviewers say about guilt (counted 19 November 2026)

A dated count behind the "Who it is for" lines in the README.

## Method
- **Source:** Apple's public customer-review feed for the US App Store, `https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=N/id=APP_ID/sortBy=mostRecent/xml`, pages 1 to 10 (50 reviews a page, the most the feed serves).
- **Apps:** Forest: Focus for Productivity (id 866450515) and Focus Friend, by Hank Green (id 6742278016).
- **Sample:** the 500 most recent reviews of each. Forest: 14 March to 17 November 2026 (251 of them one or two stars). Focus Friend: 25 August to 17 November 2026 (8 of them one or two stars).
- **Match:** title or body mentions a dead or killed tree, guilt, shame, punishment, or feeling worse; every match was then read by hand and sorted below. Reviewer names are not recorded.

## Results
| App | Reviews read | What the matches say | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus Friend | 500 | Praise it for never making them feel guilty or ashamed, or for rewards rather than punishments (all five stars) | **8** |
| Forest | 500 | Lost a tree they did not kill: the app ended or "killed" it during a session they finished or never left | **4** (three of them one or two stars) |
| Forest | 251 one and two stars | Complain mainly about the September 2026 redesign and the subscription prompts, not about guilt | most |

Two of the Focus Friend reviews, quoted exactly:
- "I don’t have the guilt of them dying or losing health. When I do break it, they are so forgiving" (five stars, 29 September 2026)
- "It is one of the few tools I have used that doesn’t get me trapped in a shame spiral" (five stars, 21 October 2026)

And one from Forest: "I’m completing my focus sessions, and you’re still randomly killing my trees. 10 in a week!" (one star, 22 September 2026)

Reviews appear on each app's App Store page: [Focus Friend](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6742278016), [Forest](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id866450515).

## What it does and does not show
It shows that people who choose a gentle focus app name the absence of guilt as the reason, unprompted, and that a punishing mechanic also lands on people who did nothing wrong. It does not measure how many people quit Forest over guilt: Forest's recent one-star reviews are dominated by its redesign.
