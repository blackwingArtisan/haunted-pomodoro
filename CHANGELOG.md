# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Pitch.** The README and the first-visit card lead with the forgiveness stance; the README adds sourced research, a comparison with Forest and Focus Friend, the Costume Contest fit and a plan beyond the hackathon. `docs/demo-video.md` follows the new beat sheet; `docs/submission.md` holds the submission copy.
- **Records.** The task list and the Kiro log record the measured end-to-end results (23 of 23; the house-file test 10 of 10 repeated runs) instead of "written, not run".
- **Phone layout.** At 720 px and narrower the ghost speaks in a caption under the scene, the demo banner fits one line with a short Leave button, and toolbar labels are visually hidden rather than removed, so every icon button keeps its name. A new axe scan runs at 390 px. Stats, streak and number inputs use lining tabular numerals.
- **The restoration as the hero moment.** The demo parlor now starts fully haunted and one 20-second candle restores it, so the whole change from cold to warm happens on screen. The drapes part over a title card, a new candle flares, and a restoration sweeps warm light and dust across the room. By the third wandering the ghost grows to 1.8x and tints the room's edges. Reduced motion shows stills.
- **Story and voice.** The ghost's journal in the house map gives back one page of her unfinished book per restored room, with a finale after six. The ghost has fifteen return lines chosen by room and wander count (up from four), and the Hearth gains a diary line and a weekly reflection. All of it is data and pure functions in `src/core/story.ts`, with a unit test that keeps every line free of shaming words.
- **Secondary screens.** The house map leads with the exterior across a band of night sky, with restored windows glowing and flickering. On the Hearth, unlit days are ghosted candle outlines, and a new house invites the first candle.
- **Media.** A twelve-second hero GIF at the top of the README and a 1200x630 link-preview image, both recorded from the real demo by `npm run media`. `index.html` gains Open Graph and Twitter card tags; `SITE_URL` makes the image link absolute at build time.
- **Candle flare.** The flare when a candle lights is now a soft radial glow rather than a flat disc.
- **Traceability.** `npm run trace` maps each of the 147 acceptance criteria to the tests that name it and publishes the table in the spec folder; 138 are covered. New tests cover auto-starting focus, choosing a task, the ghost going back to sleep, the tab title coming back, the opening title card, the candle flare, color returning mid-session, the Hearth's numbers, synthesized-only sound, four live-region announcements, the focus ring, shortcut keys while typing and the demo banner.
