# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **A week to show, and a postcard to share.** `?demo=week` opens a demo house with two made-up weeks of history, so the weekly reflection ("time away fell from 55 min to 13 min") and the 14-day chart have something to show; the Hearth comes first and the banner links to it. The Hearth's new **Save this week's postcard** button saves a 1200x630 PNG of the house from outside, with a lamp in every restored window, the week's candles and comebacks and a line from the ghost.
- **The candle pays off.** The struck flame now flares larger, and a warm pool of candlelight spreads from the mantel over the wall and the rug as the session runs, so the room warms visibly minute by minute and stays warm once restored. A haunted room is eerier: its edges are darker, and about every 19 seconds lightning flashes twice in the window and along the moonbeam (never under reduced motion).
- **A bigger stage.** On a desktop the timer moves to the top of the right-hand column, above the ledger, so the stage takes the whole left column (about two thirds of the width at 1440x900) and nothing covers the rug or the Restored caption. On a phone the stage runs edge to edge at 280 px tall, each toolbar icon has a word under it, a long task name is cut short in the "Working on" list, and the skip button reads "Skip" while still announcing what it skips. Small capitals are at least 13 px, secondary text is brighter, and the ghost's words hang beside her, clear of the portrait and the clock face.
- **Unmask is a costume change.** The drapes close over the stage while the ghost tugs her sheet, and open on the plain view about 1.1 s later; putting the costume back on drops the sheet and ends on a candle strike. Reduced motion swaps at once. In the plain view the timer loses its glow, brass ornaments and engraved labels and sits below the view instead of over it, the tiles are sized to their content with the session's progress as one wide card, and the whole timer fits the first view at 1440x900.
- **Comebacks, not wanderings.** The timer reads "Came back N times", the Hearth counts comebacks today, and the plain view names the ghost as the comeback counter. Each day now logs its time away (older saves simply have none), and the weekly reflection reports the number that should fall, time away: "6 comebacks; time away fell from 14 min to 6 min."
- **A shorter README.** About 1,000 words: the Judges box, what sets it apart, the costume, Built with Kiro, the audience and its research, the Forest comparison with a desk-scope line (Forest's Chrome extension, checked November 2026), and a plan led by a study-hall house for groups, priced against Forest's current subscription. The full feature list moved to `docs/FEATURES.md`.
- **The Kiro write-up.** `docs/submission.md` gains "How we used Kiro", by feature; the README gains a one-line Kiro summary; `KIRO-LOG.md` gains a feature table (first real use, session, evidence) and names Claude Code as the tool behind the work done outside Kiro.
- **Moonlight and a lived-in house map.** The haunted parlor now reads as cold moonlight rather than sepia (no sepia, more contrast, a stronger blue cast and moon shaft, brighter cobwebs). On the house map, the room being restored shows the ghost in its window instead of a dim amber fill that looked lit, haunted windows gather ivy, restored rooms spill warm light onto the ground, and the house gains porch steps and a picket fence. The README media is re-recorded to match, and `npm run media` now cuts the link-preview image from the restored stage rather than a full-window still, and catches both restored frames early in the break so a busy machine cannot carry them into the next room.
- **Fresh media.** `npm run media` now records the README stills too (haunted, the ghost after a wandering, restored, costume off and the finale) alongside the hero GIF, which now includes the wander-and-return beat, and the link-preview image.
- **README for a first-time reader.** A 60-second path for judges, what sets the timer apart, the lead audience first, "Built with Kiro" as a table of what each piece actually did, a "Why now" and a unit-economics line with sources. Build, test and architecture notes moved to `docs/DEVELOPING.md`.
- **The finale demo.** `?demo=finale` opens the demo house on its last dark room. One 20-second session restores the Observatory, the ghost finishes her book, and the house map opens on the last page with a new, dark house down the lane and the finished one lit up the hill.
- **Fixed:** a shortcut pressed straight after closing a window with Escape, or right after erasing the house, was sometimes ignored. Dialogs now close on the `cancel` event and ignore a late `close` event.
- **Unmask.** Press U, or the Unmask button beside the room heading, to take the costume off: the same timer, tasks and history as a plain Pomodoro, with a note beside each number naming the part of the house that stands for it. Press it again and the house is back, exactly where it was.
- **Will-o'-wisps.** Every time you wander off during focus and come back, the ghost leaves a small light near the ceiling: one per return that day, up to eight. They are never taken away, they survive the end of the session and a reload, and the scene's label describes them as the times you came back.
- **Kiro configuration.** `.kiro/settings/mcp.json` adds the Playwright MCP server and Context7 at pinned versions. Four new hooks: a PreToolUse guard (`scripts/kiro/guard.mjs`) that blocks dependency changes, `git push` and deploys with exit code 2; a PostTaskExec trace check; a SessionStart list of open spec tasks (`scripts/kiro/open-tasks.mjs`); and a Manual demo-ready check (`npm run demo-ready`). Two file-match steering files, `scene-art.md` and `audio.md`, and a read-only `ghost-reviewer` custom agent. Unit tests run the guard the way Kiro does and check every hook, agent, steering and MCP file; `docs/kiro/hook-checks.txt` records the hook commands run by hand.
- **A clear payoff frame.** After a restoration the Restored caption sits on the rug and the ghost speaks beside the window only once the sweep has finished, so the portrait and the fireplace stay in view. The timer plaque overlaps the stage by 24 px instead of 64. Lighting a candle washes the whole stage in a 600 ms warm bloom (a plain fade under reduced motion), the mantel candles are drawn 1.6x larger, and the haunted room keeps 8% of its color so it reads as moonlit rather than muddy.
- **Phone first view.** At 480 px and narrower the masthead is one line, the demo button reads "Try the 30 s demo", and a first visit shows a one-line introduction above the room that links to the full guide.
- **Accessibility.** Task checkboxes and footer links are at least 24 px tall, and the house map's decorative windows are hidden from assistive technology (the room list is the keyboard way to choose a room).
- **Pitch.** The README and the first-visit card lead with the forgiveness stance; the README adds sourced research, a comparison with Forest and Focus Friend, the Costume Contest fit and a plan beyond the hackathon. `docs/demo-video.md` follows the new beat sheet; `docs/submission.md` holds the submission copy.
- **Records.** The task list and the Kiro log record the measured end-to-end results (23 of 23; the house-file test 10 of 10 repeated runs) instead of "written, not run".
- **Phone layout.** At 720 px and narrower the ghost speaks in a caption under the scene, the demo banner fits one line with a short Leave button, and toolbar labels are visually hidden rather than removed, so every icon button keeps its name. A new axe scan runs at 390 px. Stats, streak and number inputs use lining tabular numerals.
- **The restoration as the hero moment.** The demo parlor now starts fully haunted and one 20-second candle restores it, so the whole change from cold to warm happens on screen. The drapes part over a title card, a new candle flares, and a restoration sweeps warm light and dust across the room. By the third wandering the ghost grows to 1.8x and tints the room's edges. Reduced motion shows stills.
- **Story and voice.** The ghost's journal in the house map gives back one page of her unfinished book per restored room, with a finale after six. The ghost has fifteen return lines chosen by room and wander count (up from four), and the Hearth gains a diary line and a weekly reflection. All of it is data and pure functions in `src/core/story.ts`, with a unit test that keeps every line free of shaming words.
- **Secondary screens.** The house map leads with the exterior across a band of night sky, with restored windows glowing and flickering. On the Hearth, unlit days are ghosted candle outlines, and a new house invites the first candle.
- **Media.** A twelve-second hero GIF at the top of the README and a 1200x630 link-preview image, both recorded from the real demo by `npm run media`. `index.html` gains Open Graph and Twitter card tags; `SITE_URL` makes the image link absolute at build time.
- **Candle flare.** The flare when a candle lights is now a soft radial glow rather than a flat disc.
- **Traceability.** `npm run trace` maps each of the 147 acceptance criteria to the tests that name it and publishes the table in the spec folder; 138 are covered. New tests cover auto-starting focus, choosing a task, the ghost going back to sleep, the tab title coming back, the opening title card, the candle flare, color returning mid-session, the Hearth's numbers, synthesized-only sound, four live-region announcements, the focus ring, shortcut keys while typing and the demo banner.
- **Smoke tests.** `LIVE_URL=... npm run smoke` runs three Playwright checks against a deployed copy: the page and its static files, the demo restoration, and offline reload.
